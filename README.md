# BLACK BUTTERFLY 
**Black Butterfly** is a 2017 thriller movie starring Antonio Banderas.

# What do i like in this movie?
- Because of the plot twist at the end of the movie.
- The plot of the story, like it has a very unique plot than the other movies I watched.
- Its very confusing at the start, but all those questioning will be answered through out the story.

# Quick Story 
- It's about a writer named **Paul** who have a wife and a child, one day when they are on the picnic they suddenly dissappear and because of this tradegy he can't focus on writing a novel/story. With that he leaves his job and starts into hunting to forget the past. Then one day, He heard some news that theres a killer roaming in his town, so while driving on his way home after hunting he met a guy name **Jack** and the story began.
